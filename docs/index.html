Questions, answered

Sample content
How long does a typical project take?

Most imagined projects run two to six weeks, depending on scope and how quickly feedback arrives.

Do you work with small teams and startups?

Yes. Our fictional studio is built for small, ambitious teams that want a distinctive site without the agency overhead.

Will my site be accessible?

Accessibility is part of the brief, not an add-on: semantic markup, strong contrast, keyboard support and reduced-motion respect.

Can you update an existing site instead of starting over?

Often, yes. We audit what you have, keep what works and rebuild only what holds you back.

Is any of this real?

No. Webly, its clients, numbers and answers are invented for this portfolio demo.
